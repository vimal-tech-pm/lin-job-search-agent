# Third-party credits

Lin's two resume engines are vendored, adapted forks of excellent MIT-licensed open-source projects. Their LICENSE files ship unmodified inside each engine directory, and every local divergence is tracked append-only in `LIN-LOCAL-CHANGES.md`.

| Engine | Upstream | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FORGE** (`vault-template/engines/forge/`) — narrative resume restructuring, interview coaching | [noamseg/interview-coach-skill](https://github.com/noamseg/interview-coach-skill) | Noam Segal | MIT |
| **PATHFINDER** (`vault-template/engines/pathfinder/`) — ATS keyword tailoring, A–G role evaluation, PDF rendering | [santifer/career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops) | Santiago Fernández de Valderrama | MIT |

Lin runs on [Hermes](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) by Nous Research.
